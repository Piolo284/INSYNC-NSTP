One system, every role

Everyone in NSTP works from the same records.

Trainees use the Android app, officers run sessions from their phones, and administrators manage the program from the web portal — all on one live database.

CWTS Students

Scan in to sessions, turn in modules and quizzes, track attendance and grades, and follow brigade announcements.

ROTC Cadets

The same tools for the ROTC track, plus an Inclusive Learning Section for cadets with a PWD or medical exemption.

Officers

Brigade Commanders, Platoon Leaders and Attendance Officers open QR sessions, grade submissions and review absence contests.

Administrators

CWTS and ROTC admins manage the masterlist, officers, grading weights, certificates and reports from the web portal.

QR attendance

Scan, verify, done — no paper sign-in sheets.

Officers open a timed QR session. Trainees scan it, confirm with a one-time code sent to their MARSU email, and are marked Present or Late automatically.

1
Check-in and check-out sessions
Missed check-outs are swept to Absent when the day is finalized.
2
Contest an absence in the app
Attach proof; officers approve it as Present or Excused, or reject it with a note.
3
Early warnings on standing
Trainees nearing the absence limit see a warning; officers see them on an at-risk list.

How it works

From enrollment to completion in three steps.

01

Register

Sign up with your MARSU email, campus and program. You're matched to the enrollment masterlist.

02

Train

Scan in to every session, finish modules and quizzes, and join brigade or company activities.

03

Complete

Your grade is computed from your records, and your completion certificate is issued by the program.
